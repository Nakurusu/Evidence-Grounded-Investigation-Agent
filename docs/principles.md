# Product Principles

## Principle 1

Do not claim absolute root causes.

The system identifies:

- Signals
- Drivers
- Hypotheses
- Evidence

not guaranteed causation.

---

## Principle 2

Data quality comes before analysis.

Poor data quality reduces confidence in all conclusions.

---

## Principle 3

Finding problems is more valuable than reporting metrics.

The goal is not:

- Top product
- Average revenue

The goal is:

- Discover risks
- Discover opportunities
- Discover anomalies

---

## Principle 4

Help users narrow the search space.

Reduce:

1000 possible explanations

to

3-5 highly probable explanations.

---

## Principle 5

Make investigations explainable.

Every conclusion should include:

- Evidence
- Reasoning
- Confidence

---

## Principle 6

Support arbitrary datasets.

The system should reason from data characteristics rather than fixed business schemas whenever possible.

---

## Principle 7

Recommend next actions.

A good investigation does not end with a conclusion.

It should suggest:

- What to verify next
- What data is missing
- What should be collected
